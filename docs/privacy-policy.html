Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Geography Quiz ("the App", also called GeoQuiz) is developed and maintained by the app developer ("we", "us", or "our"). This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

Information We Collect

We do not run any servers for the App, and we do not collect, receive or sell your personal information. Quiz gameplay works offline.

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Quiz scores and completion history
  • Achievement progress
  • Challenges you have sent or received
  • A quiz in progress, so you can resume it
  • App settings and preferences (e.g., timer, hard mode)
  • Player name (if you choose to set one)
  • Whether you have bought "Remove ads"

If you share a challenge, the link contains your player name, the quiz and your score, and is sent through the app you choose to share it with. Only share it with people you want to see it.

The App uses Google services for achievements, advertising and purchases, described below. Those services are provided by Google, which may process some information under its own policies.

Google Play Games Services

The App integrates with Google Play Games Services for achievements and leaderboards. Signing in to Play Games is optional; the App works without it. When you use this feature, Google may collect data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your Google account information beyond what is required for achievements and leaderboards.

Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. The App is used by people of all ages, so ads are set up the same way for everyone:

  • Every ad request is marked as child-directed and as coming from a user under the age of consent.
  • Ads are limited to content rated G (suitable for general audiences).
  • Ads are not personalised. The App does not request or use your device's advertising ID.

To show, limit and measure non-personalised ads and to prevent fraud, Google may still process limited technical information, such as your IP address and device and app information. Where required by law in your region, the App asks for consent using Google's consent tool, and you can review or change your choices at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the App. For more information, see Google's Advertising Policies and Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not have access to any personal data collected by AdMob.

In-App Purchases

The App offers one optional in-app purchase, "Remove ads". Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details. The App only stores, on your device, whether ads have been removed, and can restore the purchase from Google Play. Google's handling of purchase information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, all provided by Google:

  • Google Play Games Services — for achievements and leaderboards (as described above)
  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform — for serving advertisements and asking for consent where required (as described above)
  • Google Play Billing — for the "Remove ads" purchase (as described above)

No other data is shared with third parties.

Internet Access

The App can function fully offline for quiz gameplay. Internet access is used for Google Play Games Services (achievements, leaderboards and sign-in), for loading advertisements and consent forms, and for purchasing or restoring "Remove ads" through Google Play.

Children's Privacy

The App is an educational geography quiz for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and the App does not ask for your age. Because the App may be used by children, ads are child-directed and non-personalised for every user, as described above. Play Games sign-in is optional and is provided and governed by Google under its own terms and privacy policy.

Data Deletion

Your App data is stored on your device. You can delete it at any time:

  • In the App: go to Settings → Reset all data. This deletes your quiz history, challenges, saved quiz, achievement progress, settings and player name from your device. Your "Remove ads" purchase is kept (it can also be restored from Google Play).
  • On your device: clear the App's data in your device settings, or uninstall the App.
  • Google Play Games: achievements and leaderboard scores already sent to Google Play Games are stored in your Google account and cannot be removed by the App. You can manage or delete them in your Google Play Games account settings.

If you have a question about deleting your data, contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, please open an issue on the App's GitHub repository. Please do not include personal information in a public issue.
